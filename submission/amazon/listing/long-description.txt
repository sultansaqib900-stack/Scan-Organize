Less paper. More order.

Scan & Organize turns your compatible Fire tablet into a straightforward document scanner and local document library. Keep receipts, letters and everyday paperwork together without creating an account inside the app.

CAPTURE AND CLEAN UP
Use the camera or choose a document photo. Adjust four corners by hand to crop and straighten the page, improve contrast and sharpness, or choose black and white. Review the result and retake a page before saving.

BUILD MULTI-PAGE DOCUMENTS
Add more pages, drag thumbnails into order and remove pages you do not need. Give the document a useful name and choose its folder before saving.

KEEP PAPERWORK ORGANIZED
Create, rename and delete folders. Browse real document thumbnails, search document names across folders, and swipe through saved pages. Rename or delete documents when needed. Deleting a folder moves its documents to the default folder rather than deleting them.

EXPORT ONE PDF
Combine the pages into a single PDF and use the native file-save picker. If no system save picker is available, the app offers native sharing. Available destinations depend on the tablet and its installed providers. There is no in-app subscription or PDF-export purchase.

LOCAL DOCUMENT PROCESSING
Scanning, image cleanup, organization and PDF creation run locally on a compatible tablet. The app does not automatically upload your document library or provide cloud synchronization. Export important documents: clearing app data, uninstalling or losing the device can remove the local library. Other apps you choose when saving or sharing handle those exported copies under their own practices.

ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Pay for the app through the Amazon Appstore before download. All listed app features are included, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertising SDK integrated by the publisher.

COMPATIBILITY AND LIMITS
For touchscreen Fire tablets running Fire OS 7/8 with Amazon System WebView based on Chromium 111 or newer. A Fire OS version alone does not guarantee WebView compatibility. Fire TV, Kindle e-readers and Fire OS 5/6 are not supported by this build.

Manual crop; no OCR, automatic edge detection or post-save page editing. Up to 40 pages per document, with images resized to a 2,200-pixel longest edge. Photo imports are limited to 30 MB each. JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported; HEIC support depends on the device. Unsaved drafts can be lost if the operating system closes the app.

See the privacy policy for local storage, exports, support and separate Amazon Appstore/platform processing.
